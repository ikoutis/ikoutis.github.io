Course notes · Unit I · Meetings 1–9

There is no textbook to buy in CS 301: these notes are the course’s text, written by the instructors and posted as each meeting is reached. They follow the meetings themselves — including questions and examples that came up in class — and, together with the assignment notebook you build, they are what the exams are set from. Logistics, grading, and the schedule live in the syllabus on Canvas.

Unit I asks one question: a logic gate is written; could a neuron be taught? From logic gates to a single unit that is trained rather than written, built from scratch.
